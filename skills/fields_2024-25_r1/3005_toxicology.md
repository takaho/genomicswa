# Toxicology: research-article prose style (2024–2025)

Use this to rewrite Introduction or Discussion prose so that it reads like a current Toxicology article. Change style only: never change numbers, statistics, citations, abbreviations, technical terms, claims or the subject matter.

Evidence: 300 open-access Toxicology articles (2024–2025) compared with 90 life-science fields. Targets are this field's averages; "all fields" gives the cross-field average for contrast.

## Introduction: what to change

- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 18.0 per 300 words (all fields about 20.2 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y").
- **Vocabulary variety: more than other fields** — target MATTR about 0.73 (all fields MATTR about 0.72). How: vary wording; avoid repeating the same noun or verb in neighbouring sentences.
- **Findings/data/study as subject: more than other fields** — target about 1.0 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.8 in 10 clause subjects). How: make findings/data/results/this study the subject ("These findings suggest…").

Typical introduction phrasing in Toxicology (use where it fits the content): "The most …"; "The study …"; "Since the …"; "While the …"; "To the …"; "As the …"; "It was …"; "Additionally it …"

## Discussion: what to change

- **Word length: more than other fields** — target about 5.78 characters per word (all fields about 5.68 characters per word). How: prefer precise technical and Latinate terms and noun phrases.
- **Vocabulary variety: more than other fields** — target MATTR about 0.72 (all fields MATTR about 0.71). How: vary wording; avoid repeating the same noun or verb in neighbouring sentences.
- **Summary connectives: less than other fields** — target about 0.2 per 300 words (all fields about 0.3 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: together, collectively, taken together, overall, in summary, in conclusion.

Typical discussion phrasing in Toxicology (use where it fits the content): "However more …"; "As for …"; "There have …"; "Our analysis …"; "In particular …"; "For these …"; "Some of …"; "Therefore further …"

## Vocabulary
- Words often added by AI polishing (additionally, notably, crucial, comprehensive, insights, enhance, enhancing, robust, underscores, underscore) occur about 1.0 times per 300 words in Toxicology; do not exceed that.

## Procedure
1. Identify which of the targets above the excerpt misses.
2. Rewrite sentence by sentence, moving each of those features toward its target and leaving the rest unchanged.
3. Check that every number, [REF] marker, abbreviation and technical term is still present and that no claim was added or removed.
