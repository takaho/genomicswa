# Virology: research-article prose style (2024–2025)

Use this to rewrite Introduction or Discussion prose so that it reads like a current Virology article. Change style only: never change numbers, statistics, citations, abbreviations, technical terms, claims or the subject matter.

Evidence: 300 open-access Virology articles (2024–2025) compared with 90 life-science fields. Targets are this field's averages; "all fields" gives the cross-field average for contrast.

## Introduction: what to change

- **Organisms (mice, plants, strains) as subject: more than other fields** — target about 0.2 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.1 in 10 clause subjects). How: make the organism (mice, plants, strains) the subject where the content allows.
- **Findings/data/study as subject: less than other fields** — target about 0.7 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.8 in 10 clause subjects). How: make the entity or "we" the subject instead of data/results/study.
- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 18.5 per 300 words (all fields about 20.2 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y").
- **"We" as subject: more than other fields** — target about 0.5 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.4 in 10 clause subjects). How: make "we" the subject of what the study did or found ("We found…", "Here, we show…").

Typical introduction phrasing in Virology (use where it fits the content): "Here we …"; "The first …"; "As of …"; "In this …"; "We found …"; "Our results …"; "We previously …"; "This was …"

## Discussion: what to change

- **Organisms (mice, plants, strains) as subject: more than other fields** — target about 0.1 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.1 in 10 clause subjects). How: make the organism (mice, plants, strains) the subject where the content allows.
- **Patients/participants as subject: less than other fields** — target about 0.1 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.3 in 10 clause subjects). How: foreground the biology or variable rather than patients/participants.
- **Sentence length: more than other fields** — target about 26 words per sentence (all fields about 25 words per sentence). How: join closely related short sentences with subordinate clauses (which, whereas, because).

Typical discussion phrasing in Virology (use where it fits the content): "Here we …"; "Previously we …"; "We propose …"; "It will …"; "These data …"; "Because of …"; "We were …"; "Collectively our …"

## Vocabulary
- Words often added by AI polishing (additionally, notably, crucial, insights, enhance, robust, comprehensive, enhancing, underscores, elucidate) occur about 0.8 times per 300 words in Virology; do not exceed that.

## Procedure
1. Identify which of the targets above the excerpt misses.
2. Rewrite sentence by sentence, moving each of those features toward its target and leaving the rest unchanged.
3. Check that every number, [REF] marker, abbreviation and technical term is still present and that no claim was added or removed.
