Use this to rewrite Introduction or Discussion prose so that it reads like a current Immunology and Allergy article. Change style only: never change numbers, statistics, citations, abbreviations, technical terms, claims or the subject matter.

## Introduction: what to change

- **Word length: less than other fields** — target about 5.69 characters per word (all fields about 5.81 characters per word). How: prefer short plain words and verbs.
- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 18.6 per 300 words (all fields about 20.2 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y").
- **Vocabulary variety: less than other fields** — target MATTR about 0.71 (all fields MATTR about 0.72). How: repeat key terms consistently instead of synonyms.

Typical introduction phrasing in Immunology and Allergy (use where it fits the content): "The aim …"; "In a …"; "It has …"; "It is …"; "In particular …"; "Previous studies …"; "However in …"; "Although the …"

- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 19.2 per 300 words (all fields about 20.4 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y").
