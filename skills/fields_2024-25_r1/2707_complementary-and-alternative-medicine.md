# Complementary and alternative medicine: research-article prose style (2024–2025)

Use this to rewrite Introduction or Discussion prose so that it reads like a current Complementary and alternative medicine article. Change style only: never change numbers, statistics, citations, abbreviations, technical terms, claims or the subject matter.

Evidence: 300 open-access Complementary and alternative medicine articles (2024–2025) compared with 90 life-science fields. Targets are this field's averages; "all fields" gives the cross-field average for contrast.

## Introduction: what to change

- **Word length: more than other fields** — target about 5.91 characters per word (all fields about 5.81 characters per word). How: prefer precise technical and Latinate terms and noun phrases.
- **Sentence length: less than other fields** — target about 24 words per sentence (all fields about 25 words per sentence). How: split long sentences; one idea per sentence.
- **Modal hedges: less than other fields** — target about 0.7 per 300 words (all fields about 0.9 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: may, might, could, would.

Typical introduction phrasing in Complementary and alternative medicine (use where it fits the content): "It is …"; "Therefore it …"; "Furthermore the …"; "Studies have …"; "In recent …"; "Previous studies …"; "Therefore the …"; "Furthermore it …"

## Discussion: what to change

- **Contrast connectives: less than other fields** — target about 0.9 per 300 words (all fields about 1.3 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: however, although, whereas, in contrast, nevertheless, despite.
- **Booster verbs: more than other fields** — target about 2.2 per 300 words (all fields about 1.9 per 300 words). How: use more of: show, demonstrate, reveal, confirm, establish, highlight.
- **Hedging adverbs: less than other fields** — target about 0.7 per 300 words (all fields about 1.0 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: likely, possibly, probably, potentially, generally, relatively, largely, often.
- **Findings/data/study as subject: more than other fields** — target about 1.5 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 1.3 in 10 clause subjects). How: make findings/data/results/this study the subject ("These findings suggest…").
- **Word length: more than other fields** — target about 5.77 characters per word (all fields about 5.68 characters per word). How: prefer precise technical and Latinate terms and noun phrases.
- **Modal hedges: less than other fields** — target about 1.7 per 300 words (all fields about 2.0 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: may, might, could, would.

Typical discussion phrasing in Complementary and alternative medicine (use where it fits the content): "The results …"; "To further …"; "Studies have …"; "It can …"; "The findings …"; "It was …"; "From the …"; "The present …"; "…effects in the…"

## Vocabulary
- Words often added by AI polishing (additionally, crucial, enhance, notably, comprehensive, enhancing, insights, enhances, elucidate, robust) occur about 1.0 times per 300 words in Complementary and alternative medicine; do not exceed that.

3. Check that every number, [REF] marker, abbreviation and technical term is still present and that no claim was added or removed.
