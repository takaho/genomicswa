# Urology: research-article prose style (2024–2025)

Use this to rewrite Introduction or Discussion prose so that it reads like a current Urology article. Change style only: never change numbers, statistics, citations, abbreviations, technical terms, claims or the subject matter.

Evidence: 300 open-access Urology articles (2024–2025) compared with 90 life-science fields. Targets are this field's averages; "all fields" gives the cross-field average for contrast.

## Introduction: what to change

- **Importance words: less than other fields** — target about 0.8 per 300 words (all fields about 1.1 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: critical, crucial, key, essential, important(ly), fundamental, vital, major.
- **Contrast connectives: more than other fields** — target about 1.5 per 300 words (all fields about 1.2 per 300 words). How: use more of: however, although, whereas, in contrast, nevertheless, despite.
- **Aim statements: more than other fields** — target about 1.1 per 300 words (all fields about 0.9 per 300 words). How: state the aim explicitly: "Here, we…", "In this study…", "We aimed to…", "To investigate/determine/assess…".

Typical introduction phrasing in Urology (use where it fits the content): "To assess …"; "In our …"; "The aim …"; "The most …"; "There are …"; "Additionally we …"; "Although there …"; "Despite its …"; "…the second most…"; "…is the second most…"

## Discussion: what to change

- **Sentence length: less than other fields** — target about 23 words per sentence (all fields about 25 words per sentence). How: split long sentences; one idea per sentence.
- **Limitation statements: more than other fields** — target about 0.4 per 300 words (all fields about 0.3 per 300 words). How: where the excerpt mentions constraints, name them as limitations: "A limitation of…", "should be interpreted with caution", "cannot rule out", "may not generalize".
- **Patients/participants as subject: more than other fields** — target about 0.4 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.3 in 10 clause subjects). How: make patients/participants/individuals the subject where the content allows.
- **Contrast connectives: more than other fields** — target about 1.5 per 300 words (all fields about 1.3 per 300 words). How: use more of: however, although, whereas, in contrast, nevertheless, despite.

Typical discussion phrasing in Urology (use where it fits the content): "In our …"; "Our study …"; "First this …"; "They found …"; "The most …"; "In their …"; "The first …"; "A further …"

## Vocabulary
- Words often added by AI polishing (additionally, crucial, notably, comprehensive, enhance, insights, enhancing, robust, underscores, enhances) occur about 0.8 times per 300 words in Urology; do not exceed that.

## Procedure
1. Identify which of the targets above the excerpt misses.
2. Rewrite sentence by sentence, moving each of those features toward its target and leaving the rest unchanged.
3. Check that every number, [REF] marker, abbreviation and technical term is still present and that no claim was added or removed.
