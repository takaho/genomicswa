- **Hedging adverbs: more than other fields** — target about 1.1 per 300 words (all fields about 0.9 per 300 words). How: use more of: likely, possibly, probably, potentially, generally, relatively, largely, often.

## Discussion: what to change (most important first)

- **Sentence length: less than other fields** — target about 24 words per sentence (all fields about 25 words per sentence). How: split long sentences; one idea per sentence. Example: "X, which was measured in all samples and which increased with age, was associated with Y." → "X was measured in all samples. It increased with age and was associated with Y.". - **Summary connectives: more than other fields** — target about 0.4 per 300 words (all fields about 0.3 per 300 words). How: use more of: together, collectively, taken together, overall, in summary, in conclusion.
