## General rules (learned from earlier rewriting runs)
- Change only the features listed below, in the direction and by the amount shown. Leave everything else as in the original.
- For features marked "Apply in several places", earlier rewrites changed too little: count the feature in the original and change it throughout the excerpt, not just once.

## Introduction: what to change (most important first)

- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 19.1 per 300 words (all fields about 20.2 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y"). Example: "Activation of X by Y alters cell proliferation" → "Y activates X, which changes how cells grow". Apply in several places.
- **Vocabulary variety: more than other fields** — target MATTR about 0.73 (all fields MATTR about 0.72). How: vary wording; avoid repeating the same noun or verb in neighbouring sentences. Example: avoid repeating "increase" in neighbouring sentences: "rise", "elevation", "higher levels". Apply in several places.
- **Findings/data/study as subject: less than other fields** — target about 0.7 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 0.8 in 10 clause subjects). How: make the entity or "we" the subject instead of data/results/study. Example: "These data indicate that X binds Y" → "X binds Y". Apply in several places.
- Context only (do not force): patients/participants as subject is more common in Clinical Biochemistry; method/model/approach as subject is less common in Clinical Biochemistry. Where the original already contains such content, phrase it in the field's way.
