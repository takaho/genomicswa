## General rules (learned from earlier rewriting runs)
- Change only the features listed below, in the direction and by the amount shown. Leave everything else as in the original.

How: make the entity or "we" the subject instead of data/results/study. Example: "These data indicate that X binds Y" → "X binds Y". Apply in several places.
- **Vocabulary variety: more than other fields** — target MATTR about 0.74 (all fields MATTR about 0.72). How: vary wording; avoid repeating the same noun or verb in neighbouring sentences. Example: avoid repeating "increase" in neighbouring sentences: "rise", "elevation", "higher levels". Apply in several places.
- **Modal hedges: less than other fields** — target about 0.5 per 300 words (all fields about 0.9 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: may, might, could, would. Example: "X may explain Y" → "X explains Y" (only where the excerpt states it as a finding). Apply in several places.

Importantly, we focused on compounds that have already undergone or are currently undergoing clinical trials, ensuring that their safety and pharmacokinetic properties are at least partially characterized. We also sought compounds active against Gram-negative pathogens residing in acidified intracellular compartments."
