## Introduction: what to change (most important first)

- **Word length: less than other fields** — target about 5.69 characters per word (all fields about 5.81 characters per word). How: prefer short plain words and verbs. Example: "X increases upon inhibition of Y" → "X goes up when Y is blocked". Apply in several places.
- **Causal connectives: less than other fields** — target about 0.5 per 300 words (all fields about 0.7 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: therefore, thus, hence, consequently, because, as a result. Do not overshoot: stop at the target (at most about 1 per 300 words).
- **Importance words: less than other fields** — target about 0.9 per 300 words (all fields about 1.1 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: critical, crucial, key, essential, important(ly), fundamental, vital, major. Example: "X plays a crucial role in Y" → "X regulates Y". Apply in several places.
- **Nominalisations (-tion, -ment, -ity…): less than other fields** — target about 19.0 per 300 words (all fields about 20.2 per 300 words). How: express processes as verbs ("X activates Y"). Example: "Activation of X by Y alters cell proliferation" → "Y activates X, which changes how cells grow". Apply in several places.
- **Additive connectives: less than other fields** — target about 1.0 per 300 words (all fields about 1.2 per 300 words). How: use fewer of: moreover, furthermore, in addition, also, similarly, likewise. Example: "Moreover, X…" → "X…". Apply in several places.
- Context only (do not force): patients/participants as subject is more common in Genetics (clinical). Where the original already contains such content, phrase it in the field's way.

## Discussion: what to change (most important first)

- **Word length: less than other fields** — target about 5.60 characters per word (all fields about 5.68 characters per word). How: prefer short plain words and verbs. Example: "X increases upon inhibition of Y" → "X goes up when Y is blocked". Apply in several places.
- **Biological/clinical entities as subject: less than other fields** — target about 5.4 in 10 clause subjects (all fields about 5.7 in 10 clause subjects). How: make "we", the study or the finding the subject instead of the entity. Example: "X binds Y" → "We found that X binds Y". Apply in several places.
